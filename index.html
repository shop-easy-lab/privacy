Privacy Policy

Shop Easy (com.shoeasy.app)
Effective date: 12 September 2026 · Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shop Easy Lab ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Shop Easy mobile application (the "App"). The App helps you compare grocery prices across South African supermarkets and find nearby stores. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to provide these features.

Information we collect

  • Location data (precise and approximate). With your permission, the App reads your device's GPS location to find nearby stores and to return locally relevant prices. Location is requested only when you grant the location permission; if you decline, the App still works without location-based results.
  • Your grocery list and search queries. The item names and quantities you enter are used to search store catalogues and compare prices.

The App does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.

How we use information

  • To find supermarkets near your location.
  • To search products and compare prices across stores.
  • To speed up repeat searches through temporary caching (see Retention).

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data.

How information is shared

To provide its features, the App sends data to the following services:

  • Google Places API — to locate nearby stores, the App sends store search terms and, when available, your approximate location to Google. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Our search service — your grocery items and, when available, your location coordinates are sent to our backend (hosted on Amazon Web Services in the EU — eu-north-1, Sweden) to return prices from supermarket catalogues.

We do not otherwise share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

Data retention

  • On your device: your grocery list and last-known location are stored locally in the App and remain until you clear them or uninstall the App.
  • On our servers: to make searches faster, results keyed by store, query, and coarse location may be cached temporarily and are automatically deleted after approximately 30 days.

Location permission

The App requests the ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permissions solely to provide location-based store and price results. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Shop Easy → Permissions), and the App will continue to function without location features.

Data security

Data in transit is protected using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your rights and data deletion

You can delete all data stored on your device by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of any cached data associated with your use, contact us at the email below and we will action your request.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through a notice in the App.
